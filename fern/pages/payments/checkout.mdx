title: Checkout

Every payment link has a checkout page at `/pay/<payment link id>` on your server. It needs no login and no API key: anyone with the link can pay it. It offers the chains the link's app accepts.

The left side shows what's being paid for: the app, the title, line items and the total. The right side takes the payment.

![Checkout: the order on the left and the choice of payment method on the right](/assets/screenshots/checkout-method.png)

## Paying by sending tokens

This works with any token on any chain the app accepts, from any wallet or exchange.

<Steps>

<Step title="Pick a network and token">

After **Payment instructions**, customers pick a network and token. They can search, or paste a token's address to pay with one that isn't listed.

![Choosing the token to pay with](/assets/screenshots/checkout-token.png)

</Step>

<Step title="Review the quote">

The review shows the amount in that token, and the quote holds for 5 minutes. The customer gives a **refund address**, where the funds go if the payment can't be completed. It should be a wallet they control, not an exchange deposit address.

![The review step: quote countdown, token, network, amount and refund address](/assets/screenshots/checkout-review.png)

</Step>

<Step title="Send exactly the amount">

The customer sends exactly the amount shown to the address, using the QR code or the address as text. The QR code fills in the token, network and amount in wallets that support payment requests ([EIP-681](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-681)); the address tab is there for everything else.

![Payment instructions with a QR code for the address](/assets/screenshots/checkout-instructions.png)

</Step>

<Step title="See the result">

The page follows the transfer, and shows the result once it settles.

![Payment complete, with a link to the settlement transaction](/assets/screenshots/checkout-complete.png)

</Step>

</Steps>

## Paying from a wallet

**Connect wallet** pays from a browser wallet in one step. The checkout opens the transfer with the connected wallet as the refund address, then asks the wallet to send the quoted amount to the payment's address. It's an ordinary token transfer, so the customer pays the network's gas, as they would when sending from any wallet.

Gasless payments with [x402](/payments/x402) are for HTTP clients, such as agents, that request the payment link directly rather than opening the page.

## On your own page

Send customers to `/pay/<id>`, or keep them on your page by showing `/pay/<id>?embed` in an iframe. The embedded page posts `ready`, `status`, `success`, `failed` and `close` messages, each with `source: "kite"`, to the page around it.

## Payment windows

A transfer stays open until its quote expires. If nothing arrives in time, it's cancelled and the page offers to start over. If the customer sends less than the amount, or pays after the window, the funds are refunded to their refund address.
