---
title: Payouts
description: Receive every payment as one token on one chain, through NEAR Intents.
---

By default, a payment settles to the app's address on the chain it was paid on. An app that takes payments on several chains ends up with money on each of them. A payout delivers every payment as one token on one chain instead, such as USDC on NEAR, through [NEAR Intents](https://docs.near-intents.org/).

## Setting a payout

Under **Apps**, create or edit the app, then open **Additional information**:

![Editing an app, with its payout token and address under Additional information](/assets/screenshots/app-edit.png)

- **Payout token**: the token to receive, grouped by the chain it's on.
- **Payout address**: an address on that token's chain, such as `shop.near` on NEAR.

Set both, or neither. Kite checks the destination when you save, with a trial quote that moves no money, and 1Click refuses an address it can't deliver to.

Through the API, set `payoutAsset` and `payoutAddress` on `POST /api/apps` or `PATCH /api/apps`. `GET /api/tokens/payouts` lists the tokens NEAR Intents can deliver, with each one's `assetId`.

## What happens to a payment

1. **The transfer opens.** Kite asks NEAR Intents' 1Click API for a quote that converts the paying chain's settlement token into the payout token, delivered to the payout address. Refunds go to the app's address on the paying chain.
2. **The customer pays.** The payment's contract settles straight into the quote's deposit address. Kite never holds the money: the contract pays NEAR Intents, and NEAR Intents pays you.
3. **NEAR Intents delivers.** Kite follows the payout until it's delivered, refunded or failed, and writes each outcome to the transfer's timeline.

When there's nothing to convert, because the payout token is the paying chain's settlement token, the payment settles straight to the payout address.

## When a payment isn't paid out

A payment settles to the app's address on the paying chain, as it would without a payout, when:

- NEAR Intents doesn't support the paying chain or its settlement token. The chains it supports are listed under `descriptors` in the server's [chain registry](https://github.com/Kite-01/server/blob/main/src/shared/chains/registry.ts);
- 1Click can't quote the payment when the transfer opens.

A deposit address is valid for a day. If a payment would settle into one less than 30 minutes before its deadline, Kite doesn't send it: the transfer fails and the customer is refunded.

## Following a payout

A paid-out transfer shows its payout on the transfer's page: the destination, the status and, once delivered, the delivery transaction.

![A settled transfer paid out as USDC on NEAR, with the delivery transaction](/assets/screenshots/transfer-payout.png)

| Status       | Meaning                                                               |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Pending`    | NEAR Intents hasn't seen the deposit yet.                             |
| `Processing` | NEAR Intents has the deposit and is converting it.                    |
| `Delivered`  | The payout token reached the payout address.                          |
| `Refunded`   | NEAR Intents sent the funds back to the app's address on the paying chain. |
| `Failed`     | NEAR Intents couldn't deliver.                                        |

Payout steps are written to the transfer's timeline, but they don't send webhook events. Your webhooks hear about the transfer settling, as for any payment.

## Fees and limits

- Without a NEAR Intents API key, 1Click charges an extra fee on every payout. Set a key under **Providers**; see [Providers](/server/providers#payouts).
- NEAR Intents has no testnet, so payouts only happen on mainnet servers.
