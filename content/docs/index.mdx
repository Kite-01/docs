---
title: Kite
description: Crypto payment links and checkout. Customers pay in any token; you're paid in USDC.
---

Kite gives you payment links and a checkout for crypto. You create a link with a price in dollars, your customer pays it in any token you accept, and the payment settles in USDC. Your systems hear about it through a signed webhook.

![The Kite dashboard: amount processed, settled transfers, volume and recent transfers](/screenshots/overview.png)
