---

With the server running, everything else happens in the dashboard. This walks through it in order. By the end you'll have a chain customers can pay on, an app, an API key and a payment link.

<div className="fd-steps">

1. **Sign in**

   Open your server's URL and sign in with your `KITE_ROOT_KEY`. Tick **Remember me** to stay signed in on this browser.

   ![The sign-in screen, asking for the root key](/screenshots/login.png)

2. **Add provider keys**

   Under **Providers**, add keys for a deposit notifier, QuickNode or Alchemy, so the server hears about payments the moment they land, and for Alchemy as gas sponsor, which pays the operator's gas. Every chain needs both. [Providers](/server/providers/) explains each.

   ![The Providers page: Alchemy as gas sponsor and deposit notifier, and QuickNode as deposit notifier](/screenshots/providers.png)

3. **Add a chain**

   Under **Chains**, click **Add chain** and pick a network. The list only shows networks your server supports; a [testnet server](/server/testnets/) shows testnets.

   ![The Add chain dialog with a network picker](/screenshots/chain-add.png)

4. **Configure the chain**

   Open the chain. In **Tokens**, add the tokens customers may pay with, then in **Configuration** set your Kite factory's address, the settlement token, a deposit notifier, a gas sponsor and the rest. [Chains and tokens](/server/chains-and-tokens/) covers every field.

   ![A configured chain: factory address, settlement and USD-pegged tokens, slippage, settlement address, RPC URLs and providers](/screenshots/chain-detail.png)

   The chain shows as **Configured** once it has everything it needs.

   ![The Chains list, with Base Sepolia configured and Sepolia still needing configuration](/screenshots/chains.png)

5. **Create an app**

   Under **Apps**, create one for your business or project. Its name, icon and links are shown to customers at checkout. Open it to manage everything that belongs to it.

   ![The Apps page listing Acme Store and Northwind Coffee](/screenshots/apps.png)

6. **Create an API key**

   Inside the app, go to **API keys** and create one for your backend. See [API keys](/authentication/api-keys/).

7. **Create a payment link** and open it

   Under **Payment links**, create one and use **Open checkout** to see the page your customers get.

   ![Checkout for the Team starter pack: line items and total on the left, payment options on the right](/screenshots/checkout-method.png)

</div>

<Callout type="info">
  Inside an app, a banner reminds you which app you're changing. **Back to
  apps** returns to the server-wide pages.
</Callout>

## The overview

The dashboard's home shows the amount processed, settled transfers, customers, volume over time, top tokens and recent transfers, for the whole server or, inside an app, for that app.
